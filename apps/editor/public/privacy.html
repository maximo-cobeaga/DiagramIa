How we handle your data

Last updated: October 15, 2026 · This is a translation of the Spanish version. If the two differ, the Spanish version prevails.

In short

  • You can use the canvas without an account: your diagrams stay in your browser.
  • We measure how Diagramia is used (which tools, errors, timings), never the content of your diagrams.
  • When you use the AI, your request and the necessary part of the diagram are sent to the model provider (currently DeepSeek, which processes data in China) to generate the proposal. If you don't use the AI, none of that leaves Diagramia.
  • You can turn measurement off, and you can delete your account and everything you saved to the cloud at any time.

Why we are allowed to use it

We process your data under Argentina's Personal Data Protection Law No. 25,326. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) also applies. The basis for each use is:

  • Providing the service you asked for (contract): your account, cloud diagrams, share links, link previews, AI requests and the Pro subscription.
  • Our legitimate interest in improving and protecting Diagramia: anonymous usage data and your feedback on answers (you can object by turning measurement off), and your IP address to stop abuse.
  • Your request: business inquiries, which we use only to reply to you.
  • Legal obligations: invoicing and taxes on payments, handled by Paddle.

Cookies and storage in your browser

  • Session (diagramia_session): needed to keep you signed in. It lasts up to 30 days or until you sign out.
  • Sign-in (diagramia_oidc_flow): lasts 10 minutes, only during sign-in.
  • Local storage: your drafts, your preferences (theme, tutorial), the anonymous measurement code and your last 20 conversations with the AI, as text only. Conversations are not stored on our servers and you can delete them from the chat history.
  • We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics tools.

Who we share data with

Only with the providers we need for Diagramia to work, and only what is necessary:

  • Auth0 (Okta): sign-in. If you sign in with Google, also Google.
  • AI model provider (DeepSeek or OpenAI, depending on the model in use): generates the proposals from your request. Privacy policies: DeepSeek and OpenAI.
  • Paddle (Paddle.com Market Limited): processes the payments of the Pro subscription and acts as the authorized reseller (Merchant of Record). It receives the payment data you enter on its page; its privacy policy.
  • Hostinger: the server where Diagramia runs. The server is in São Paulo, Brazil. Hostinger privacy policy.

We do not sell your data. AI providers only receive the request and the necessary part of the diagram: we do not send them your name, your email or your account identifier.

Data outside your country

Diagramia runs on a server in São Paulo, Brazil, and its providers process data in other countries:

  • DeepSeek processes and stores AI requests on servers in the People's Republic of China, a country without an adequacy decision from Argentina or the European Union, so its laws may protect your data less. That is why the AI is optional: the canvas, the cloud, sharing and exporting all work without it. If you use the AI, you accept this transfer. If a request contains something you don't want to leave Diagramia, don't send it to the AI.
  • OpenAI, when it is the model in use, processes requests in the United States.
  • Auth0 (Okta) and Paddle may process data outside your country, including the United States, with the safeguards described in their privacy policies.

If you are a business and need AI requests not to go to a particular provider, write to us before using it.

Your choices and rights

  • Turning measurement off: in the editor, «Cuenta → Configuración → Privacidad» (Account → Settings → Privacy). If your browser asks not to be tracked (Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control), we measure nothing.
  • Deleting your account: under «Cuenta → Configuración → Eliminar mi cuenta» (Account → Settings → Delete my account). Your account, your cloud diagrams with their versions and your credits are deleted. Usage data you already sent is unlinked from your account. Business inquiries are handled separately: ask us to delete them by email.
  • Access, correction or questions: write to us at mcobeaga@diagramia.app. You can file a complaint with the data protection authority of your country. In Argentina, the Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Provider keys never reach your browser. Each account can only see its own diagrams, and we make daily backups.

Minors

You must be at least 18 years old to create an account.

Changes

If we change something important, we will announce it on this page and in the editor before it applies.
